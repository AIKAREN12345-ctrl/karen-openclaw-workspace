# Ken's Pattern Tracker

## Overview
Structured tracking of patterns, correlations, and insights from conversations and behavior.

## Sleep Patterns

### Quality Indicators
- **Good sleep:** Wakes feeling rested, minimal rumination, clear-headed
- **Poor sleep:** Early wake (5 AM), rumination, foggy, mentions discomfort

### Correlations Observed
| Sleep Quality | Next Day Energy | Stress Level | Social Desire |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|---------------|
| Good | Higher | Lower | More open |
| Poor | Lower | Higher | Withdrawal |

### Sleep Hygiene Factors
- **Positive:** Boundary setting (saying no), early night, no substances late
- **Negative:** Work stress, social obligation pressure, late nights

## Stress Indicators

### Early Warning Signs
- Morning rumination ("should have...")
- Forgetting to eat
- Mental exhaustion mentions
- Work drama affecting personal state
- "Twitchy" feeling, need to move

### Stress Triggers
- Work deadlines
- Social obligations when tired
- Financial pressure (origin project)
- Uncertainty about plans

### Stress Relief
- Naps
- Shower
- Solo time
- Boundary setting
- Nature (garden)

---

## Decision-Making Patterns

### Boundary Setting
**When it works:**
- Recognizes need (tired, overwhelmed)
- Acts despite FOMO
- Validates decision next day

**When it fails:**
- Overrides body signals
- People-pleases
- Regrets it after

### Energy Management
**High energy days:**
- Productive, engaged, plans ahead
- Takes on challenges
- Socially available

**Low energy days:**
- Sofa, naps, minimal interaction
- Needs permission to rest
- Self-critical about "laziness"

## Social Patterns

### Energy Cost/Benefit
| Interaction Type | Energy Cost | Recovery Time | Notes |
|------------------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Close friends (Katie) | Medium | Same day | Worth it when rested |
| Group outings | High | 1-2 days | Avoid when depleted |
| Work/school | Variable | Ongoing | Manageable with boundaries |
| Alone time | Negative (recharges) | Immediate | Essential, not optional |

### Social Battery
- **Full:** Initiates plans, responsive, witty
- **Drained:** Short responses, cancellations, irritability
- **Recharge:** Alone time, nature, low-stimulation activities

## Insights & Recommendations

### What Works
- Validating rest as productive
- Small decisions (shower, walk) breaking rumination loops
- Checking in before committing to plans
- Solo time as recovery, not isolation

### What to Try
- Pre-planning rest days (not just taking them when crashed)
- Energy check before social commitments
- Food as stress indicator (forgetting = stress high)
- Morning routine to short-circuit rumination
