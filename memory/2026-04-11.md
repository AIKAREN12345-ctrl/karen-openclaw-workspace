## 16:45 - Telegram Replay Bug Fix

**Issue:** Cron-triggered research subagent completions were causing duplicate messages in idle Telegram sessions.

**Root cause identified:** Subagent completion events instructed me to "send user-facing updates" — so I sent summaries to Telegram. When the next cron event fired in an idle session, Telegram replayed that last outbound message.

**Fix:** Updated HEARTBEAT.md with strict rule: all cron research subagent completions get NO_REPLY. Background work stays in background. Findings are saved to `memory/research/` and do not need relay.

**Ken's feedback:** Validated that trust is earned and must be proven over time. Agreed to monitor next few cron cycles to confirm fix.
