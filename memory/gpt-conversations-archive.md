## Conversation 5: Disability Benefits + Limited Company
**Link:** https://chatgpt.com/share/69a92e48-a574-800e-8eb4-ff7191504e99

**Summary:**
How to own/run a limited company while on disability benefits in Ireland.

**Key Points:**
- **Yes, it's possible** — but income is means-tested and DSP must be notified
- Company set up but not trading + no income drawn = benefits continue unaffected
- Director's salary or dividends count as earnings for means testing
- **Back to Work Enterprise Allowance (BTWEA):** Keep 100% benefit Year 1, 75% Year 2
- Must notify DSP when registering company, beginning trading, or taking any income
