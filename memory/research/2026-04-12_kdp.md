# KDP Coloring Books Trends 2026

**Date:** 2026-04-12

## Findings

1. **"Bold and Easy" is the #1 growth trend in 2026** — large, simple outlines with thick lines appeal to beginners, seniors, and stress-relief seekers, commanding adult pricing ($8.99–$12.99) with 50–70% less production time than intricate designs.

2. **Niche-specific books massively outperform generic titles** — a generic mandala book faces 50,000+ competitors and earns ~$200/month, while a niche mandala book (e.g., zodiac theme) has ~200 competitors and earns ~$1,200/month.

3. **Underserved blue-ocean niches for 2026 include cottagecore, dark academia, pet portraits, and medical/anatomy coloring books** — these have under 200 competitors, passionate audiences, and support premium pricing ($10–$18).
