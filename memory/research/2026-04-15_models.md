## Models for 24GB RAM

### 1. GLM-4.7-Flash (Reasoning)
- **Size:** 30B parameters (A3B MoE)
- **RAM needed:** ~20-23GB VRAM (Q4_K_M quant)
- **Use case:** Best overall for coding & agentic workflows. 30.1 Intelligence Index, won agentic coding challenge. 42 tok/s speed.

### 2. Qwen3 30B A3B
- **Size:** 30B parameters
- **RAM needed:** ~20-23GB VRAM (Q4_K_M quant)
- **Use case:** Best for long-context reliability. Maintains 33-53 tok/s at 48K tokens with zero offloading. Stable for document processing.

### 3. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano 30B A3B (Reasoning)
- **Size:** 30B parameters
- **RAM needed:** ~22-23GB VRAM (Q4_K_M quant)
- **Use case:** Best for mathematics & scientific reasoning. 91.0 Math Index, 75.7 GPQA score. Highest math performance in 24GB tier.

---
**Note:** BitNet 1.58 models (1.58-bit quantization) enable running larger models with less VRAM. Microsoft's bitnet-b1.58-2B-4T uses only ~0.5-1.5GB VRAM, leaving massive headroom for 70B+ models at extreme quantization on 24GB systems.
