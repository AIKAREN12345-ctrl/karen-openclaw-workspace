#### Key Success Factors

1. **Pick ONE service** - not three, not five
2. **Specialize** - generalists compete on price, specialists on value
3. **Build proof** - 2-3 demo projects with Loom walkthroughs
4. **First client at 50% rate** - case study worth more than revenue
5. **Productize services** - fixed-price packages vs hourly billing
6. **Build direct client relationships** - avoid platform fees long-term
