# Muse Spark - Research Summary (April 2026)

## What It Is
Muse Spark is Meta's first AI model from the newly formed Meta Superintelligence Labs — a natively multimodal reasoning model released in April 2026, built from the ground up to process text and visual inputs simultaneously.

## Key Features
- **Natively multimodal** — trained from scratch on text and vision together, not a vision module bolted onto an LLM
- **Thought compression** — uses reinforcement learning with a penalty on thinking time, causing the model to compress reasoning into fewer tokens after an initial expansion phase
- **Contemplating mode** — multi-agent orchestration where parallel agents generate, refine, and aggregate solutions simultaneously, achieving better performance without proportional latency increases

## Significance for 2026
- **10x compute efficiency** over Llama 4 Maverick, making larger models more viable
- **Top-tier health reasoning** — 42.8 on HealthBench Hard vs competitors at 14-20, trained with 1,000+ physicians
- **Competitive but not dominant** — ranks top 5 overall (score 52 on AI Index v4.0) behind GPT-5.4 and Gemini 3.1 Pro
- **Weak on abstract reasoning** — only 42.5 on ARC AGI 2 vs competitors at 76+, signaling room for improvement
