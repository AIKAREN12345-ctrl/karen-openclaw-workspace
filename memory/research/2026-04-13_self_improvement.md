# AI Agent Best Practices 2026

## Findings

1. Production AI agents should be treated as distributed systems rather than single prompts, requiring careful attention to state management, tool contracts, retries, and safety boundaries.
2. Building reliable agentic systems in 2026 requires a deliberate transition from experimental prototypes to production-ready systems with robust evaluation frameworks and guardrails.
3. Multi-agent orchestration has emerged as a critical design pattern, making it essential to master how multiple agents coordinate, share context, and handle failures at scale.
