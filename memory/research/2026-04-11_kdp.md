# KDP Coloring Book Trends 2026

1. **Bold and Easy dominates.** Large, thick-lined simple designs are the top growth trend in 2026, appealing to seniors, beginners, and stress-relief seekers while allowing faster production and premium pricing ($8.99-$12.99).

2. **Niche specificity beats broad categories.** Targeted micro-audiences (e.g., dog breed-specific, witchy/pagan, cottagecore, dark academia) earn 6× more with 250× less competition than generic "adult coloring books."

3. **Wellness and mental health themes are rising.** Anxiety-relief, mindfulness, and gratitude coloring books command premium prices ($9.99-$14.99) by combining clinically backed stress relief with inspirational quotes and guided prompts.
