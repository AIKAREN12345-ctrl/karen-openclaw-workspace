**3. AI-Generated Digital Templates**

## Brief Summary of Opportunities

### Easiest to Start (Low Barrier):
1. **AI Content Writing** - Use ChatGPT/Claude to offer blog writing services
2. **Social Media Management** - Use AI tools to manage multiple client accounts
3. **Digital Template Sales** - Create once, sell repeatedly on Etsy

### Highest Income Potential:
1. **AI Automation Services** - Premium rates for business automation
2. **AI Content Agencies** - Serve businesses with high marketing budgets
3. **Faceless YouTube Channels** - Scale to multiple channels

### Most Passive:
1. **Digital Products** - Templates, courses, prompt libraries
2. **Affiliate Marketing** - Promote AI tools with recurring commissions
3. **Automated Newsletters** - Set up once, monetize ongoing

### Key Insight for 2026:
The barrier to entry has never been lower. AI eliminates the need for technical expertise, expensive equipment, or large teams. Success depends on:
- Choosing a niche with demand
- Using AI to scale output
- Maintaining human quality control
- Building systems that run with minimal ongoing effort

**Recommended Starting Point:** Pick ONE area (content writing, video, or automation), master the relevant AI tools, automate the workflow, then expand.
