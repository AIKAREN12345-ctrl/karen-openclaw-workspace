## Security Research - 2026-04-14 17:20

- **Indefinite data retention and weak access controls are the top AI security risks for 2026**, requiring organizations to implement short retention windows, automated purging, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to prevent unauthorized access to training data and reduce insider risk. (Source: Brooks Kushman)

- **2026 marks a shift from policy-based to evidence-based privacy compliance**, with regulators expecting operational controls including automated data maps, AI risk assessments, and documented governance as enforcement periods end and penalties increase. (Source: Cloud Security Alliance)

- **U.S. states are leading AI regulation in 2026** with Colorado's AI Act (effective Feb 1), California's AI Transparency Act (effective Jan 1), and Texas's TRAIGA requiring impact assessments, transparency disclosures, and documented AI lifecycle management for high-risk systems. (Source: MIND)
