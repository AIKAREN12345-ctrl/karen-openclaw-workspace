# Local LLM Developments & Optimization — 2026

- QLoRA improvements and frameworks like Unsloth now make fine-tuning 8B-parameter models feasible on a single 12GB consumer GPU, democratizing local LLM customization for small teams and individual developers.
- Self-hosted LLMs are increasingly cost-competitive against cloud APIs in 2026 for steady, high-volume workloads, especially when data privacy, compliance, and vendor independence are priorities.
- Go 1.26 integrations with local runtimes (e.g., NVIDIA TensorRT LLM via Triton Inference Server) are maturing, enabling high-performance, low-latency inference applications with robust concurrency and production monitoring.
