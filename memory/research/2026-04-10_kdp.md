# KDP Coloring Books Trends 2026

## 1. Bold and Easy dominates
Large, thick-lined simple designs for beginners/seniors are the biggest growth trend on Amazon KDP in 2026, commanding adult pricing ($8.99-$12.99) with faster production time and lower return rates.

## 2. Niche specificity wins over broad categories
Generic "adult coloring books" are saturated (50,000+ competitors), while micro-niche titles like zodiac mandalas or breed-specific dog books earn 6x more with 250x less competition.

## 3. Mindfulness Patterns + vector quality are emerging
A new anti-anxiety style of simple outlines filled with repeating geometric patterns is gaining traction, and professional vectorization (300 DPI crisp lines) is the key differentiator against low-quality AI-generated competition.
