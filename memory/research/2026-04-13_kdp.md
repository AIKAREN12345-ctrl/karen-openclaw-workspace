# KDP Coloring Books Trends 2026

**Research Date:** 2026-04-13

## Key Findings

1. **Mindfulness mandalas remain a top profitable niche** for KDP coloring books in 2026, continuing the trend toward stress-relief and wellness content.

2. **Adult fantasy themes** are identified as one of the most profitable niches, indicating strong demand for imaginative, escapist content.

3. **Eco-friendly nature designs** are trending, reflecting growing consumer interest in sustainability and environmental themes.
