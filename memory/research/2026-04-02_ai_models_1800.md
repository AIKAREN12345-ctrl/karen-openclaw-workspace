# AI Model News - April 2, 2026

*Research conducted: April 2, 2026 at 18:00*

## Key Findings

### 1. OpenAI Closes Record $122B Funding Round
- **Valuation**: $852 billion (largest single fundraise in venture history)
- **Key Investors**: Amazon, Nvidia, and SoftBank anchored $110B of the raise
- **Revenue**: OpenAI reports $2B/month revenue, claiming 4x growth pace vs Alphabet and Meta at same stage
- **Source**: [Rundown.ai](https://www.rundown.ai/articles/openai-new-122b-funding-superapp)

### 2. OpenAI Plans "AI Superapp" Integration
- OpenAI confirmed plans to fold ChatGPT app, coding platform Codex, and browser into a single desktop "superapp"
- Goal is to simplify user experience and create unified AI workspace
- Represents strategic shift toward platform consolidation
- **Source**: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai/)

### 3. Anthropic Claude Code Source Code Leak
- Significant security breach involving leaked source code for Claude AI coding agent
- Leak reportedly revealed internal product logic, unreleased features, and implementation details
- Anthropic states no customer data or credentials were exposed, and core model weights were not leaked
- Incident highlights increasing competition and security challenges in AI industry
- **Source**: [Humai.blog](https://www.humai.blog/ai-news-trends-april-2026-complete-monthly-digest/)

### 4. OpenAI Shutting Down Sora Video Model
- OpenAI is shutting down Sora, its AI video generation model/app
- Shutdown comes despite regular updates being released through the week prior
- Decision appears abrupt and may signal strategic repositioning
- **Source**: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-is-shutting-down-sora-its-powerful-ai-video-app)

### 5. Industry Shift: From Growth to Monetization
- AI industry entering phase of consolidation and consequence in April 2026
- Gap between demo and production results defining winners and losers
- Open-weight models narrowing gap to frontier systems, affecting enterprise procurement
- Regulatory frameworks in EU moving from draft to enforcement
- **Source**: [Humai.blog](https://www.humai.blog/ai-news-trends-april-2026-complete-monthly-digest/)
