## Core Insights

### On Suffering:
- Suffering comes from identifying with mental stories
- The narrator in your head isn't who you are
- Notice thoughts and feelings as they arise
- Don't become the story

### On Presence:
- Presence is the awakened state of consciousness
- It transcends ego and discursive thinking
- It's available now, not in the future
- It's the end of suffering

### On Practice:
- Simple body-based practices reduce reactivity
- Carry less emotional weight
- Pause regularly throughout the day
- Use the body as an anchor to now
