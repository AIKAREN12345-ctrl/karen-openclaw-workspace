## Current Trends (April 2026)

### #1 Trend: Bold and Easy Coloring Books
- **Style**: Large, simple outlines with thick lines (3-5px), minimal intricate detail
- **Audience**: Beginners, seniors, people with limited dexterity, stress-relief seekers
- **Pricing**: $8.99-$12.99 (commands adult pricing despite simpler designs)
- **Why it works**: 50-70% faster to produce than intricate designs, broader appeal, lower return rates
- **Sub-niches**: Bold and Easy Flowers, Animals, Landscapes, Seniors, Holiday themes, Breed-specific pets

### Trending Niches for 2026
1. **Cottagecore / Cozy Aesthetic** - Under 200 books, $9.99-$12.99 pricing
   - Mushroom foraging, cozy cabins, wildflowers, tea cups, bread baking
2. **Dark Academia** - Under 50 books, $10.99-$13.99 pricing
   - Old libraries, gothic architecture, vintage books, botanical illustrations
3. **Witchy/Pagan/Wiccan** - 200-500 competitors, $10-15 pricing
   - Moon phases, herbs, crystals, tarot (TikTok #WitchTok effect)
4. **True Crime (Noir aesthetic)** - Under 100 books, $9-12 pricing
   - Detective/noir aesthetic, crime scene tape patterns (avoid actual crime references)
5. **Zodiac/Astrology** - 400-700 per sign, $8-11 pricing
   - 12 signs = 12 potential books + compilations

## Validation Framework

### 7-Point Niche Validation Checklist
Before creating, verify at least 5 of 7:

1. **Search volume**: 200-2,000 Amazon results (sweet spot)
2. **Competition quality**: Amateur covers = opportunity
3. **BSR check**: At least 3 top-10 results under 100,000 BSR
4. **Review analysis**: 2-3 books with 50+ reviews
5. **Price viability**: Top sellers at $8.99+
6. **Community check**: 5,000+ members in Facebook groups/Reddit
7. **Trend direction**: Stable or growing on Google Trends

**Scoring**: 7/7 = launch immediately | 5-6/7 = strong opportunity | 3-4/7 = proceed with caution
