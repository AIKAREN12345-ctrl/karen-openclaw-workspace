## 1. Qwen3.5 (Alibaba) - Released March 2026

**Source:** https://ollama.com/library/qwen3.5  
**Hugging Face Activity:** High - Multiple Qwen3.5 variants being uploaded

### Overview
Qwen 3.5 is a family of open-source multimodal models that delivers exceptional utility and performance. Released approximately 1 week ago (as of March 13, 2026).

### Available Sizes
- 0.8B, 2B, 4B, 9B, 27B, 35B, 122B parameters

### Key Capabilities
- **Vision:** Yes
- **Tools:** Yes
- **Thinking:** Yes
- **Cloud:** Yes (larger variants)

### Highlights
- **Unified Vision-Language Foundation:** Early fusion training on multimodal tokens achieves cross-generational parity with Qwen3 and outperforms Qwen3-VL models
- **Efficient Hybrid Architecture:** Gated Delta Networks combined with sparse Mixture-of-Experts deliver high-throughput inference
- **Scalable RL Generalization:** Reinforcement learning scaled across million-agent environments
- **Global Linguistic Coverage:** Expanded support to 201 languages and dialects
- **Next-Generation Training Infrastructure:** Near-100% multimodal training efficiency

### Notable Benchmarks
- **MMLU-Pro:** 87.8 (competitive with GPT5.2 at 87.4 and Claude 4.5 Opus at 89.5)
- **LiveCodeBench v6:** 83.6
- **AIME26:** 91.3
- **MMMU (Vision):** 85.0
- **MathVision:** 88.6
- **VideoMME:** 87.5

## 2. LFM2 (Liquid AI) - Released February 2026

**Source:** https://ollama.com/library/lfm2  
**Release Date:** ~2 weeks ago (as of March 13, 2026)

### Overview
LFM2 is a family of hybrid models designed for on-device deployment. LFM2-24B-A2B is the largest model in the family.

### Available Sizes
- 24B parameters (LFM2-24B-A2B)

### Key Capabilities
- **Tools:** Yes
- **On-device deployment optimized**

### Highlights
- **Best-in-class efficiency:** 24B MoE model with only 2B active parameters per token
- **Memory efficient:** Fits in 32 GB of RAM for deployment on consumer laptops and desktops
- **Fast edge inference:** 112 tok/s decode on AMD CPU, 293 tok/s on H100
- **Predictable scaling:** Quality improves log-linearly from 350M to 24B total parameters
