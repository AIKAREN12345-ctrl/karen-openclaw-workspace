# Google Gemma 4 Research Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-13

## What is Gemma 4?
Google's most capable open AI model family, released April 2026. Built from Gemini 3 research and technology, designed to maximize intelligence-per-parameter across diverse hardware—from mobile devices to workstations.

## Key Features & Specs

- **Four model sizes:** E2B (Effective 2B), E4B (Effective 4B), 26B MoE (Mixture of Experts), and 31B Dense
- **Apache 2.0 license:** Fully open-source with permissive commercial terms—no restrictive usage barriers
- **Multimodal native:** All models process video, images, and audio (E2B/E4B); supports OCR, chart understanding, and speech recognition
- **Massive context windows:** 128K for edge models (E2B/E4B), up to 256K for larger models (26B/31B)
- **140+ languages:** Natively trained for global multilingual applications
- **Agentic capabilities:** Native function calling, structured JSON output, system instructions for autonomous workflows

## Significance for 2026

- **#3 open model globally:** Gemma 4 31B ranks third on Arena AI text leaderboard, outperforming models 20x its size
- **Edge AI breakthrough:** E2B/E4B run offline on phones, Raspberry Pi, and Jetson Nano with near-zero latency
- **Democratizes frontier AI:** Consumer GPU-friendly (quantized versions) while offering H100-class performance unquantized
- **Research accelerator:** Already driving innovations like Bulgarian-first language models (BgGPT) and cancer therapy research (Yale Cell2Sentence-Scale)
