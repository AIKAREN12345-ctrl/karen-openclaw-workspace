## Eckhart Tolle

### Key Teachings on Presence

> "Words reduce reality to something the human mind can grasp, which isn't very much."

### Core Concepts

**What is Presence?**
- The recognition of The Now is presence
- Presence arises when we free ourselves of incessant thinking and unconscious reactivity
- Rolling back the ego so our essential self can shine through
- A new way of living becomes possible with presence

**Presence in Conversation**
- Maintaining presence while talking is challenging because "the moment you start talking, the two minds come together and so they strengthen each other"
- A flow starts - a stream of thought - that can pull us out of presence
- The practice involves awakening from the "dreamlike state of continuous thought"

**Practical Applications**
- Doorways into Presence: Eckhart's most effective tools for accessing Presence in daily life situations
- Practice of Presence: Eliminating stress, anxiety and preventing feeling overwhelmed
- The Power of Presence course: A year-long immersion to awaken to your true nature
