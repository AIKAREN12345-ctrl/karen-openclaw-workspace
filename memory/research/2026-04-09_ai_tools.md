- **PaperOrchestra** (writing) — Announced April 8, 2026 by Google Cloud AI Research. A multi-agent system that autonomously converts unstructured pre-writing materials (idea summaries + raw experimental logs) into submission-ready LaTeX research papers, complete with literature reviews, generated figures, and API-verified citations. Completes in ~40 minutes and achieves simulated acceptance rates of 84% on CVPR and 81% on ICLR. Source: <https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/08/google-ai-research-introduces-paperorchestra-a-multi-agent-framework-for-automated-ai-research-paper-writing/>
