## 1. Amazon KDP Royalties Pricing Changes

### Major Change: Print Book Royalty Reduction (June 2025)

**Key Finding:** Amazon KDP reduced print book royalties from **60% to 50%** for books priced below certain regional thresholds, effective June 2025.

**Impact Analysis:**
- Books priced at **$9.99 or above** still earn **60% royalty**
- Books priced below threshold now earn **50% royalty**
- Example impact: A book previously earning $2.93 royalty (at 60%) now earns $2.03 (at 50%) — **over 30% reduction in royalty**

**Actionable Insight:**
- **Price strategy adjustment:** Price books just above the $9.99 threshold to maintain 60% royalty rate
- This represents a significant shift in KDP's pricing structure favoring higher-priced books
