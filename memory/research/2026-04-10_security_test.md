# AI Security & Privacy Best Practices for 2026

1. **Data retention & access controls:** Indefinite retention of AI training and interaction data, combined with weak access controls, creates significant exposure across privacy, cybersecurity, and regulatory enforcement—organizations should implement short retention windows, automated purging, and robust role-based access control (RBAC) tailored to AI systems.

2. **AI governance convergence:** In 2026, privacy, AI, and cybersecurity are fully converging, meaning organizations must integrate these disciplines into a single governance framework with cross-functional accountability, harmonized risk assessments, and enterprise-wide AI inventories.

3. **Vendor & third-party AI risk:** Most AI technologies are bought rather than built, so updating vendor diligence to include AI-specific risk assessments and revising contracts to address training data use, audit rights, and liability allocation are critical for managing downstream data exposure.
