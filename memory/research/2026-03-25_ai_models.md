## Industry Trends (2026)

### Key Observations:
1. **Price Crash:** AI model prices have crashed 40-80% year-over-year
2. **Benchmark Convergence:** Top models (GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro) are within 1-2 points of each other on major benchmarks
3. **Multi-Model Strategy:** Developers are increasingly running 2-3 models in parallel rather than picking sides
4. **Shift to Deployment:** Industry pivoting from "new model every week" to "how do we deploy at scale securely"
