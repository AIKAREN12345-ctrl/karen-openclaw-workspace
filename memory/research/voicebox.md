# Voicebox Research Summary

## What is Voicebox?

Voicebox is an open-source, local-first voice cloning studio — a free alternative to ElevenLabs. It allows users to clone voices from a few seconds of audio, generate natural speech in 23 languages, and compose multi-voice projects entirely on their own machine.

---

## Key Findings

- **Local-first privacy**: All models and voice data stay on the user's machine — no cloud processing, no subscriptions, complete data privacy

- **Multi-engine architecture**: Supports 5 TTS engines (Qwen3-TTS, LuxTTS, Chatterbox, Chatterbox Turbo, HumeAI TADA) with different strengths — from lightweight CPU-friendly models to high-quality multilingual voice cloning with emotion control via tags like `[laugh]`, `[sigh]`, `[gasp]`

- **Significance for 2026**: Voicebox represents a major shift toward democratized, privacy-preserving AI voice technology — giving individuals and creators full control over voice synthesis without vendor lock-in or recurring costs, while supporting 23 languages and running natively across macOS (MLX/Metal), Windows (CUDA/DirectML), Linux, and even Intel Arc/AMD ROCm
