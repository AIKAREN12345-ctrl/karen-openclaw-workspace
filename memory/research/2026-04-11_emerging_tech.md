# Emerging AI Technologies 2026

## AI as Collaborative Partner
AI is evolving from a question-answering tool into a true collaborative partner, with agents acting as digital coworkers that amplify human expertise across medicine, software development, and scientific research.

## Security for AI Agents
As AI agents proliferate in workplaces, organizations are prioritizing built-in safeguards—clear agent identities, access controls, and autonomous threat detection—to prevent them from becoming unchecked security risks.

## AI-Driven Business Value
Businesses are aggressively investing in AI training and adoption, but success in 2026 depends on overcoming infrastructure costs and workflow transformation to scale AI from individual productivity tools into organization-wide assets.
