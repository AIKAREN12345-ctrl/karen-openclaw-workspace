# AI Tools Research - April 2026

*Research Date: 2026-04-16*

## 1. Content Creation: ElevenLabs Image & Video (Beta)
ElevenLabs expanded from audio to a full creative platform, letting users generate images and videos using Veo, Sora, Kling, WAN, and Seedance models—then add voices, music, and sound effects with built-in lipsync in a single workflow.

## 2. Productivity: Salesforce Slackbot (Autonomous Work Assistant)
Salesforce upgraded Slackbot with 30 new AI features, transforming it from a simple notification tool into an autonomous work assistant that can take multi-step actions, coordinate tasks across channels, and integrate with Salesforce CRM data directly in Slack conversations.

## 3. Developer Tools: Cursor 3 (Agent-First Coding)
Cursor 3 introduces a completely new agent-first interface that lets developers assign entire coding tasks to AI agents rather than writing code directly—a fundamental shift from AI assistance to AI execution that competes directly with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex.
