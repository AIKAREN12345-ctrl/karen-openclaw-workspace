### Stoicism for Modern Life

**Key Principles:**
- Focus on what you control (effort, attitude)
- Accept what you don't (outcomes, others' actions)
- Amor fati — love of fate

**Daily Practice:**
1. Morning reflection — What challenges today?
2. Evening review — What went well? What to improve?
3. Negative visualization — Prepare for setbacks

### Decision-Making Framework

**The 10-10-10 Rule:**
- How will I feel about this in 10 minutes?
- How will I feel in 10 months?
- How will I feel in 10 years?

**When Tired/Stressed:**
- Default to rest, not decisions
- "No" is a complete sentence
- FOMO fades; burnout doesn't

### Boundary Setting

**Lessons from recent experience:**
- Saying "no" to one thing opens space for better things
- Rest is productive (recovery enables performance)
- Morning rumination shortens when boundaries honored
- Sofa days are valid recovery, not laziness

### Quote to Remember
> "The obstacle is the way." — Marcus Aurelius

Challenges aren't interruptions to the plan; they ARE the plan.
