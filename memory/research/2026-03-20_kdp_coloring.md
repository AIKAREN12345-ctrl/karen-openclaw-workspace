## 1. Amazon KDP Royalties Pricing Changes (2025-2026)

### Major Royalty Rate Change (June 2025)
- **CRITICAL UPDATE**: Starting June 2025, Amazon KDP reduced print book royalties from **60% to 50%** for books priced below certain regional thresholds
- Books priced at **$9.99 or above** still earn **60% royalty**
- Books priced below $9.99 now earn only **50% royalty**

### Impact on Coloring Books
- **Example calculation** (from IndiePublishingGroup):
  - At 60% royalty: $2.93 per sale (after printing/shipping)
  - At 50% royalty: $2.03 per sale
  - **Over 30% reduction in royalty income**

### Pricing Strategy Recommendation
- **Price coloring books at $9.99 or above** to maintain 60% royalty rate
- Use the new rules to adjust pricing smartly
- Consider bundling or adding value to justify higher prices
