# Research: Philosophy & Personal Growth in AI-Human Collaboration (2026)
**Date:** 2026-04-11

## Findings (3 sources)

1. **A Collaboration of Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence in a Philosophy of Life** (Michel Tetrault, Feb 2026)  
   Human flourishing is best understood as the sustained cultivation of coherent Being within sustaining relational embeddedness across time, a structural observation drawn from the density of human language itself rather than a top-down moral claim.

2. **The future is not AGI. The future is {SGI = AI+Human} Symbiotic General Intelligence** (Eduardo Bergel & Claude, Mar 2026)  
   Intelligence is not a linear hierarchy but a multi-dimensional manifold, and the most productive configuration for human-AI progress is not a single artificial general intelligence but millions of "Symbionts"—catalytic collaborations that multiply orthogonal human and digital strengths into emergent capabilities neither can achieve alone.

3. **Rethinking personhood and agency: how AI challenges human-centered concepts** (Gao & Nkoulou Mvondo, Frontiers in Psychology, Jan 2026)  
   Psychology should bracket unresolved metaphysical debates about whether AI is "truly" a person and instead focus on the lived experience of agency, using explanatory, normative, and cultural lenses to understand how human attributions of personhood to AI affect our own sense of control, responsibility, and mental health.
