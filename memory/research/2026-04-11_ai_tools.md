# AI Tools Research - April 11, 2026

## Google DeepMind Gemma 4
Google launched Gemma 4 (Apr 2, 2026), a family of open AI models built for advanced reasoning and agentic workflows, featuring four sizes from edge-optimized 2B/4B up to 26B MoE and 31B Dense, natively supporting function-calling, structured JSON, video/image input, and up to 256K context windows under an Apache 2.0 license.

## Google AI Edge Eloquent
Google released AI Edge Eloquent (Apr 6, 2026), a free, offline iOS dictation app that transcribes speech in real time and automatically polishes the output by removing filler words like "um," with Android and macOS versions planned.

## ToolMesh
ToolMesh launched (Apr 7, 2026) as a secure execution layer for AI agents, aiming to bridge the gap between rapidly deploying agents into production and maintaining proper security, governance, and sandboxing controls.
