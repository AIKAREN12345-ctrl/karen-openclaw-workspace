**Research Date:** March 18, 2026

## Executive Summary - Top 5 Options

### 1. TU Dublin - Civil Engineering (TU026) ⭐ BEST OVERALL
- **Level:** 6 (Higher Certificate) → 7 (BEng Tech)
- **Duration:** 3 years part-time (Level 6) + 2 years (Level 7)
- **Delivery:** Thursday evening online + Friday all day in Bolton Street
- **Entry:** Open to mature students; work experience valued
- **Progression:** Clear ladder to Level 8 Honours and Level 9 Masters
- **Cost:** SUSI eligible (free if unemployed, 10% if employed)
- **Why it fits:** Specifically designed for mature students transitioning to engineering; strong maths support; excellent employment outcomes

### 2. TU Dublin - Building Engineering Level 6 (TU018) ⭐ BEST FOR CONSTRUCTION PATH
- **Level:** 6 (Higher Certificate)
- **Duration:** 3 years part-time
- **Delivery:** One full day + one evening per week (Bolton Street)
- **Entry:** Leaving Cert or equivalent with Maths & English; mature applicants assessed individually
- **Progression:** Direct entry to Level 7 Building Engineering (TU021)
- **Cost:** SUSI eligible
- **Why it fits:** Engineers Ireland accredited; clear progression to honours degree; designed for industry professionals

### 3. TU Dublin - Electronic Engineering FLASHE (TU601) ⭐ BEST FOR TECH PATH
- **Level:** 6 (Higher Certificate)
- **Duration:** 2.5-3 years
- **Delivery:** Monday-Thursday 6pm-10pm (2 mandatory classes + 1 voluntary) + some Saturdays
- **Location:** Tallaght campus
- **Entry:** Leaving Cert with Maths O5/H7; mature students (23+) eligible
- **Progression:** To Level 7 BEng (TU726) then Level 8 BEng Hons (TU829)
- **Cost:** Standard part-time fees (SUSI eligible)
- **Why it fits:** Flexible evening schedule; strong tech industry links; shorter duration than civil/building

### 4. TU Dublin - BIM (Digital Construction) Springboard+ (TU174) ⭐ BEST SHORT-TERM UPSKILL
- **Level:** 8 (Honours Degree - add-on)
- **Duration:** 1 year part-time
- **Delivery:** Friday 1:30pm-5:30pm Bolton Street + 2 evenings online (65% online, 35% on-campus)
- **Entry:** Level 7 in built environment OR Level 6 Craft Apprenticeship + 5 years experience
- **Progression:** To MSc in Applied BIM & Management
- **Cost:** 10% of course fee if employed (Springboard+)
- **Why it fits:** Fast-track to Level 8; 95%+ employment rate; high demand for BIM skills; January 2024 BIM mandate creates job opportunities

### 5. TU Dublin - Electrical Services Engineering (TU027)
- **Level:** 7 (BEng Tech)
- **Duration:** 3 years part-time
- **Delivery:** 3 evenings per week (minimum), 1 in-person
- **Location:** Grangegorman
- **Entry:** Qualified electrician OR NFQ Level 6 Engineering OR relevant experience
- **Cost:** SUSI eligible
- **Why it fits:** Energy/renewables focus; excellent career prospects; can enter with trade background

## Alternative Pathways

### Trinity College Dublin - Mature Student Entry

**Mature Student Definition:** Over 23 years on January 1st of application year

**Entry Routes:**
1. **Direct Admission** - Apply via CAO; may require entrance exam/assessment
2. **Foundation Course for Matures** - 1-year preparation course
3. **FET/PLC Route** - Level 5/6 Major Awards accepted
4. **City of Dublin ETB Partnership** - Pearse College or Plunket College foundation courses

**FET/PLC Requirements for Trinity:**
- Minimum 5 academic distinctions (Work Experience modules don't count)
- Specific module requirements vary by course (e.g., Maths for Engineering)
- Maximum 390 points (best 8 modules counted)
- Apply via CAO by February 1st

**Contact:** Trinity Access Programmes (TAP) - Goldsmith Hall, Pearse Street

### UCD - Mature Student Entry

**Contact:** UCD Registry Admissions via Student Desk Connector

**Note:** UCD Professional Academy offers short professional courses but these are not degree programmes.

## Maths-Specific Options

### TU Dublin Maths Support
- **Maths Learning Support Centre:** Free one-to-one support for all TU Dublin students
- **Academic Writing Centre:** Free support for academic writing
- **Additional Tutorials:** Available in maths and science for part-time engineering students

### PLC Maths Options (QQI Level 5/6)
Many PLC courses offer Mathematics modules that can:
- Refresh algebra, calculus, and applied maths
- Meet entry requirements for engineering degrees
- Provide QQI points for CAO applications

**Typical PLC Courses with Strong Maths:**
- Engineering Technology (Level 5/6)
- Pre-University Engineering
- Applied Science

**Note:** Specific PLC providers in Dublin include:
- City of Dublin ETB colleges (Cathal Brugha, Pearse, Plunket)
- Dundrum College of Further Education
- Greenhills Community College

### Leaving Cert Maths (Night Classes)
Many adult education centres and ETBs offer:
- Leaving Cert Maths evening classes
- Intensive maths preparation for mature students
- Often free or low-cost

## Recommendations

### For Immediate Start (September 2026):

**If you have some technical background:**
→ Apply for **Civil Engineering (TU026)** or **Building Engineering (TU018)**
- Both welcome mature students
- Strong employment outcomes
- Clear progression pathways
- Good maths support available

**If you prefer tech/electronics:**
→ Apply for **Electronic Engineering FLASHE (TU601)**
- Shorter duration (2.5-3 years to Level 6)
- Evening schedule may suit working
- Strong industry demand

**If you need significant maths preparation first:**
→ Consider:
1. Enrol in a QQI Level 5 Maths module at a local ETB college (evening/weekend)
2. Apply for 2027 intake to engineering courses
3. Use TU Dublin's free Maths Learning Support Centre

### For Career-Changers:
The **Civil Engineering (TU026)** course is specifically designed for mature applicants and career-changers. The course description explicitly states it is "particularly aimed at mature applicants who currently work in the industry and wish to upskill, change careers or gain formal qualifications."

### Funding Strategy:
1. Apply for SUSI grant immediately when applications open
2. Register interest for Springboard+ courses as backup
3. If employed, budget for 10% contribution (typically €300-800 for Level 7)

## Additional Research Findings (Post-Timeout)

### DCU (Dublin City University)

**Mature Student Entry:**
- DCU accepts mature students (23+ by January 1st of entry year)
- Assessment based on "lifelong learning" — studies, work experience, personal interests
- Apply via CAO by **February 1st, 2026** for September 2026 entry

**Engineering at DCU:**
- DCU offers 5 specialist engineering courses + Common Entry option
- **However:** DCU appears to focus on **full-time** undergraduate programmes
- Limited part-time engineering options found for mature students
- Main pathway: Full-time degree via CAO mature entry

**Contact:**
- Mature Students Support: Part of DCU Access Service
- Glasnevin and St. Patrick's campuses
- Website: www.dcu.ie/mature-students

**Verdict:** Less suitable for your situation (needs part-time, employed status). TU Dublin part-time options are better fit.
