# AI Agent Best Practices 2026 - Research Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-10  
**Source:** Subagent research task

## Key Findings

### 1. Start Simple, Add Complexity Only When Needed
The most successful AI agent implementations use simple, composable patterns rather than complex frameworks—optimize single LLM calls first before building multi-step agentic systems.

### 2. Governance and Risk Management Are Critical
By 2027, over 40% of agentic AI projects will fail due to escalating costs or insufficient risk controls; establishing AI governance frameworks with clear decision hierarchies and security guardrails is essential for success.

### 3. Design Clear Agent-Computer Interfaces (ACI)
Tools should be designed with the same care as human-computer interfaces—include example usage, edge cases, and clear documentation to minimize errors and maximize reliability.
