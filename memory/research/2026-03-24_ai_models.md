## Key Trends & Insights

### 1. Performance Convergence
- Top models are within 1-2 points on most benchmarks
- No single "best" model - each excels in specific domains
- Claude: Best for coding and long-form codebase work
- Gemini: Best for abstract reasoning and price-performance
- GPT-5.4: Best for terminal/automation tasks and balanced performance

### 2. Price War
- Prices dropped 40-80% year-over-year
- Gemini 3.1 Pro offers premium performance at mid-tier pricing
- DeepSeek V4 disrupts with open-weights at extremely low API costs

### 3. Context Window Arms Race
- 1M token context is now standard across flagship models
- Enables whole-repo understanding and complex multi-file changes

### 4. Multi-Model Routing Strategy
- 37% of enterprises now use 5+ models in production
- Smart developers run 2-3 models in routing setups:
  - Cheap model (Gemini/Grok): Documentation, simple refactors
  - Mid-tier (GPT-5/Claude Sonnet): Feature development, debugging
  - Premium (Claude Opus 4.6): Complex architecture, intricate debugging
- IDC predicts 70% of top AI enterprises will use routing by 2028

### 5. Open-Weight Models Are Competitive
- Qwen3-Coder-Next matches Claude Sonnet on SWE-bench Pro
- MiniMax M2.5: 80.2% SWE-bench at $0.30/$1.20 per million tokens
- Performance gap between open and closed models has closed dramatically
