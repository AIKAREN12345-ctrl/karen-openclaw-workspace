## 1. Amazon KDP Royalties & Pricing Changes (2025-2026)

### Major Policy Change (Effective June 2025)
- **CRITICAL**: Amazon KDP reduced print book royalties from **60% to 50%** for books priced below certain regional thresholds
- Books priced at **$9.99 or above** still earn the full 60% royalty rate
- This significantly impacts low-priced coloring books common in the market

### Regional Thresholds
- Pricing thresholds vary by marketplace (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.)
- Check KDP's official royalty calculator to see impact on specific titles
- KDP provides a downloadable list of affected titles via Bookshelf (only visible if impacted)

### Actionable Insights
- **Pricing Strategy**: Price coloring books at $9.99+ to maintain 60% royalty rate
- For a $6.99 coloring book: losing 10% royalty = significant impact on thin margins
- Review all titles priced below threshold and consider price increases
- Use KDP's royalty calculator to model different pricing scenarios
- Factor in increased printing costs when calculating profitability
