## 1. Arcee AI Releases Trinity-Large-Thinking (399B Parameters)

**Released:** April 3, 2026  
**Source:** VentureBeat, LLM-Stats

Arcee AI, a San Francisco-based lab with just 30 employees, released **Trinity-Large-Thinking** — a 399-billion parameter text-only reasoning model under the Apache 2.0 license. This represents a significant move for "American Open Weights" as a sovereign alternative to increasingly closed frontier models.

**Key Details:**
- 399B parameter MoE (Mixture of Experts) architecture
- Full commercial usage rights
- Designed for enterprises seeking domestic AI solutions
- Available on Hugging Face
