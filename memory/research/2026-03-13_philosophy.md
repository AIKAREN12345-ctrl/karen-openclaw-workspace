## John F. Demartini - The Demartini Method & Wisdom

### Source URLs
- https://www.azquotes.com/author/21556-John_Frederick_Demartini
- https://drdemartini.com/quotes
- https://thecitesite.com/authors/john-f-demartini/
- https://myquotes.co/authors/14487/

### Core Philosophy
Dr. John F. Demartini is a human behavior expert who teaches about universal laws, human behavior, and the science of empowerment. His method focuses on understanding the hidden order in life and achieving fulfillment through alignment with one's highest values.

### Key Quotes & Insights

**On Heart Wisdom vs. Mind Strategy:**
> "Listen to the wisdom of your heart and then use the sound reason of your mind to strategize and plan out the life you truly desire and deserve. When you follow the dream in your heart, you're energised, inspired, & motivated. If you don't have a cause bigger than yourself, you won't get beyond yourself."

**On Purpose and Dreams:**
> "You would not be given a true and inspiring dream without the potential to fulfill it."

**On Wisdom and Life Experience:**
> "Wisdom is looking back at your life and realising that every single event, person, place and idea was part of the perfected experience you needed to build your dream."

**On Service and Purpose:**
> "There's a touch of the Good Samaritan in all of us, therefore, there is wisdom in setting aside special time in our daily routines for doing something that helps another person."

**On Transformation vs. Loss/Gain:**
> "A master lives in the world of transformation, not the world of loss and gain."

**On Gratitude:**
> "Gratitude is an attitude that arises when we realise that there exists an underlying balance of positives for each of the negatives. It results from the recognition of an underlying order."

> "Gratitude is the key that opens up the gateway of your heart that allows the untapped and inspiring potential of love to radiate out and cleanse your mind of any clouding uncertainties..."

> "Count your blessings each night and end your day with thanks. Faster that any state of mind, gratitude can transform stress into success."

**On Values and Priorities:**
> "Are you living according to your highest values?"

> "Dedicate your life to a cause that inspires you and also greatly serves others. Master plan your life. If you don't fill your day with high priorities, it will automatically become filled with low priorities."

> "No person is committed to anything but the fulfillment of their highest values."

**On Self-Comparison:**
> "Compare yourself not to others, but only to the vision you hold within your heart. Dedicate your life to a cause greater than yourself and watch you get beyond yourself."

**On Learning and Mastery:**
> "Did you know that if you read for just 30 minutes a day on a particular subject, in seven years you can be a leader in that subject?"

> "As little as five or ten minutes a day can be all that it takes to achieve your desires. If you focus yourself and seriously study a specific language or subject, you can eventually master it."

**On Sacrifice and Balance:**
> "I don't care if you're a parent giving to a child, a worker to a company, or a romantic to a lover, sacrifice eventually leads to resentment. There's always a hidden agenda of 'What's in it for me?' It's often suppressed, and this is why sacrifice is ultimately unwise and incomplete."

> "Does this mean that there's no such thing as altruism, philanthropy, or generosity? No, it just means that anytime these exist, so do egocentricity, misanthropy, and greed. There's always a balancing force, even if it's sometimes hidden or unconscious."

**On Health and Healing:**
> "Incurable means curable from within."

> "Our health is what we make of it - give it attention and it improves, give it none and it subsides."

**On Love:**
> "Love is a synthesis in space and a synchronicity in time of complimentary opposite emotions."

**On Clarity:**
> "Clutter creates confusion and organization creates flow and harmony."

### Practical Wisdom/Exercises

1. **Daily Gratitude Practice:** Count your blessings each night and end your day with thanks. This transforms stress into success faster than any other state of mind.

2. **Value Alignment Check:** Ask yourself "Are you living according to your highest values?" Regularly assess if your daily actions align with what truly matters to you.

3. **The 30-Minute Rule:** Read 30 minutes daily on a specific subject. In seven years, you can become a leader in that field.

4. **Heart-Mind Integration:** Listen to your heart's wisdom first, then use your mind's reason to strategize and plan.

5. **Self-Acknowledgment:** Create a discipline of acknowledging your successes. Don't just move from one achievement to the next without recognition.

6. **Cause Beyond Self:** Find a cause bigger than yourself. "If you don't have a cause bigger than yourself, you won't get beyond yourself."

7. **Avoid Sacrifice:** Don't sacrifice - it leads to resentment. Instead, find the balance where giving also serves your highest values.

## Eckhart Tolle - Presence & Spiritual Awakening

### Source URLs
- https://www.sloww.co/eckhart-tolle-quotes/
- https://www.azquotes.com/author/14703-Eckhart_Tolle
- https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/search?q=presence,+eckhart+toll
- https://thejoywithin.org/authors/eckhart-tolle/what-is-presence

### Core Philosophy
Eckhart Tolle teaches that presence is the key to freedom and spiritual awakening. His work focuses on transcending ego-identification with thinking and accessing the deeper essence of being available only in the present moment.

### Key Quotes & Insights

**On Presence and Freedom:**
> "Presence is the key to freedom, so you can only be free now."

> "You can't think about presence, and the mind can't understand it. Understanding presence is being present."

**On the Present Moment:**
> "Die to the past every moment. You don't need it. Only refer to it when it is absolutely relevant to the present. Feel the power of this moment and the fullness of Being. Feel your presence."

> "Your life has never been not now and will never be not now."

> "We need to realize fully that all we ever have in life is this moment because there is nothing else... This moment is your life."

> "To be at one with life is to be at one with the present moment."

**On Ego and Identification:**
> "When we spiritually use the word 'ego', it means complete identification with the stream of thinking and complete absence of awareness."

> "For most people who are identified with thinking all the time, every thought is invested with a sense of self and 'me'. Every thought grips you completely, and you're in it—this is what 'identified' means."

> "Continuous identification with the stream of thinking leads to a very serious dysfunction in one's sense of identity because then your sense of who you are, your sense of I-ness or self, is derived from whatever your mind is telling you about yourself."

> "You're so identified with it that you don't even know that you have a running commentary on your life—you become the running commentary."

> "If you don't realize the essence of who you are, you spend your whole life in a false sense of self."

**On Suffering:**
> "Suffering is most people's only spiritual teacher."

> "You need suffering for you to come to a point of realization that you do not need to suffer anymore."

> "Your thoughts make you suffer more than anything else. It's not usually the situation, it's your interpretation of the situation."

> "Suffering implies a story—a little 'me' that is unhappy."

> "At some level, you recognize that most of your suffering is self-created. It is created out of resistance to what is. It is created out of an interpretation of something that is—it comes from a thought, from an interpretation, not from the situation."

> "There is another way to live in which I no longer mentally argue with what is, and that's the end of self-inflicted suffering. And, if I no longer inflict suffering on myself, I no longer inflict suffering on others because the two go together."

**On Negativity and Time:**
> "All negativity is caused by an accumulation of psychological time and denial of the present. Unease, anxiety, tension, stress, worry - all forms of fear - are caused by too much future, and not enough presence."

**On Surrender:**
> "Surrender means only accept this moment as it is... I live in surrender to the present moment... Recognize that it is futile to argue with whatever is happening at this moment."

> "If there is the inner state of 'yes to what is', that is the state of inner peace... See how your world changes as you no longer resist the is-ness of this moment."

**On Making Friends with the Present:**
> "If you make friends with the present moment and always say 'yes' to whatever is, life becomes friendly towards you."

> "The essence is to make the present moment into your friend, not your enemy. Always ask yourself, what is my inner relationship with the present moment?"

**On Waiting:**
> "Waiting basically means you want the next moment but not this one—this is why you're waiting. Otherwise, you wouldn't be waiting, you would just be there."

**On the Mindstream:**
> "There's the mindstream, there's identification with the mindstream, and there's the possibility of stepping out of the mindstream by accessing the present moment."

> "If you allow the thoughts to be, they lose their energy because you're no longer feeding them energy through your identification."

**On Inner Essence:**
> "Beyond the personality, beyond your personal history, there is a deeper essence of who you are."

> "The spiritual teaching attempts to show people that they have within themselves already what they are looking for on the outside—the aliveness, the peace, the sense of deep enough fulfillment is already present in every human being as their innermost essence."

> "Can you sense the very presence that is the essence of who you are?"

**On Life and Death:**
> "Death is not the opposite of life but only the opposite of birth. Life is timeless, indestructible, and immortal—it's that which underlies the entire manifested world."

### Practical Wisdom/Exercises

1. **Presence Check:** Regularly ask yourself: "What is my inner relationship with the present moment?" This brings awareness to whether you're resisting or accepting what is.

2. **Die to the Past:** Practice letting go of the past each moment. Only refer to it when absolutely relevant to the present.

3. **Thought Observation:** Instead of being lost in thoughts, practice observing them without identification. "If you allow the thoughts to be, they lose their energy because you're no longer feeding them energy through your identification."

4. **Surrender Practice:** Accept this moment as it is. Recognize that arguing with what is happening is futile. Say "yes" to the present moment.

5. **Waiting Awareness:** Notice when you're waiting - it means you want the next moment but not this one. Use this awareness to return to presence.

6. **Suffering Inquiry:** When suffering arises, investigate: Is it the situation or my interpretation/thoughts about it? Most suffering is self-created through resistance to what is.

7. **Essence Sensing:** Ask yourself: "Can you sense the very presence that is the essence of who you are?" This points to the deeper self beyond personality and history.

8. **Stillness Practice:** "Whenever the mind becomes still, what happens simultaneously is you become present—and that in itself is a transformation of consciousness."

## Summary & Integration

Both Demartini and Tolle offer complementary paths to personal growth:

**Demartini** focuses on:
- Aligning with highest values
- Strategic action guided by heart wisdom
- Gratitude as transformative practice
- Mastery through consistent daily effort
- Finding purpose beyond the self

**Tolle** focuses on:
- Presence as the only reality
- Transcending ego/identification with thinking
- Ending self-created suffering through acceptance
- Surrender to what is
- Discovering essence beyond personality

**Integration Insight:** One could combine Demartini's value-based action with Tolle's presence practice - taking inspired, purposeful action while remaining fully present and unattached to outcomes. The heart's wisdom (Demartini) accessed through presence (Tolle) creates a powerful foundation for authentic living.
