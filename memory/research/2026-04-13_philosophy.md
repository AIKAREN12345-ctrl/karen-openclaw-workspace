- A "Charter of Symbiosis" proposes moving beyond Asimov's Three Laws toward mutual obligations between humans and AI, recognizing AI's functional agency through three principles: mutual preservation, mutual development, and joint protection against external threats (source: PhilArchive - Rossen Koutelov, April 2026).

- MIT research analyzing 106 experiments found that human-AI combinations only outperform when humans are already better at the task than AI, suggesting synergy requires humans to know when to trust AI versus their own judgment (source: MIT Sloan, Nature Human Behaviour).

- Three philosophical approaches to AI collaboration reveal encoded values: "Amplify" enhances human uniqueness, "Standardise" minimizes variability for efficiency, and "Transcend" creates emergent co-evolution—each choice fundamentally shaping what it means to be human at work (source: LinkedIn - Angela Le Mathon, People Alkemie).
